## How I work

- Every money number gets a definition and a baseline before I report it. Negotiated savings are measured against the last price paid, at the volume forecast at signing, and finance validates them. Cost avoidance (an increase I talked a supplier out of) is reported separately and never added to savings.
- A should-cost model before any negotiation on a part over $1M a year.
- Any commitment I can't cancel gets a decision memo with a demand range and the cost of the low case. I learned that one the expensive way.
- Technical calls belong to engineering and quality. I can read a datasheet and talk derating, but I don't overrule a quality engineer on a test plan anymore.
