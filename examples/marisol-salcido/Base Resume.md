## Experience

### Verdillo Power Systems — Senior Strategic Sourcing Lead, Power Electronics (Mar 2023 – present)

Tempe, AZ. Privately held maker of industrial UPS systems and power conversion systems (PCS) for commercial battery storage. $1.2B revenue and $640M direct material spend in 2025; plants in Tempe and Hermosillo, Mexico. IC role, no direct reports. I own category strategy for power semiconductors, magnetics and film capacitors ($182M annual spend in 2025, 23 suppliers) and lead cross-functional sourcing projects. 2 commodity managers and 3 buyers work with me on those projects; they report to the Director of Strategic Sourcing.

- **Magnetics should-cost and renegotiation (2023).** Our sole magnetics supplier asked for +9.5% on 14 custom parts ($21.6M a year). I built a should-cost model for the top 5 parts and closed at −3.0%, with a copper index clause and 2-year terms. $648K a year in negotiated savings against the last price paid (finance-validated); $2.05M a year of avoided increase, reported separately. Full notes: `Projects/Magnetics Should Cost Model.md`.
- **DC-link capacitor quality crisis (2024).** A supplier changed its film source without the notice our contract required, and capacitors started failing in the field. I ran the supplier side: containment, replacement supply, the claim, and a second source qualified in 7 months. Recovered $1.21M of $1.93M in costs. Full notes: `Projects/Capacitor Quality Crisis.md`.
- **S&OP (2023–2024).** Designed and ran monthly sales and operations planning for the energy storage business unit for 14 cycles; forecast accuracy 58% → 74%, raw-material days of supply 112 → 74. Full notes: `Projects/Sales And Operations Planning.md`.
- **Single-source risk review (2023–2024).** Reviewed 214 single-source parts with engineering; 37 rated critical. 26 of the 37 have a qualified alternate as of October 2026.
- **IGBT module agreements (2025–2026)** with ±25% quantity flex at 90 days' notice. We used the flex in 2025 when a customer project slipped.
