## Failures, indexed honestly

- 2021: I agreed to carve breaches of confidentiality out of a liability cap without tracing the definition. It included customer data, so the client carried uncapped liability for any data breach under that contract for 19 months. I found it myself in 2023 and told the client within the week. Full notes: `Projects/The Carve Out I Missed.md`.
- 2022, on secondment: I held a marketing page for 9 days over a claim the team could already back up. I hadn't asked who had the proof (`Projects/In House Secondment.md`).
- 2024: my rollout plan assumed every customer had notice-and-object rights for new subprocessors. 38 had negotiated prior written consent (`Projects/Privacy Program Rollout.md`).
- 2025: I asked for the open-source code scan in week 4 instead of week 1, so the biggest finding of the deal arrived in week 6 of 7 (`Projects/Acquisition Due Diligence.md`).
