## Failures, indexed honestly

- The clinic's first 8 months: a 31% no-show rate, because I designed scheduling around our convenience, not the patient's.
- The clinic's first budget case: $1.1M a year in avoided readmissions, which finance rightly didn't accept. The $310K version kept the clinic open.
- My first drug-use count in the formulary review missed 14 emergency department courses. A neurosurgeon caught it before the committee did.
- The first dosing-weight rule slowed heparin starts from 52 to 81 minutes. I hadn't tested it against the most time-critical workflow.
- The heart failure study's outcome definition changed after 60 charts, and the first journal rejected the manuscript partly for that.
