## How I read an SE offer (it is not an AE offer)

- An AE's variable rides on their own deals. Mine rides on deals other people close: today, my whole region's new ARR. So I compare offers on expected cash: base plus variable times the trailing 8-quarter payout rate of the pool it pays on. I ask for that payout history in the first conversation.
- Base is the number I negotiate. An AE should push for uncapped accelerators because they control their deal volume. I don't, so I weight base and the split instead.
- A cap of 150% or higher is fine if the base is right. Most SE plans have one.
- What the variable pays on matters. A region or team quota is what I want. One AE's number makes me an AE without the commission. A global pool means my work doesn't move my pay.
- SE credit should come from being SE of record in the CRM once a deal reaches technical validation. It should not depend on an AE adding me to a split.
- An MBO piece (for example POC conversion) is fine if it's 20% of the variable or less and defined in writing.

## Where I have real depth

## Search constraints — please respect these

- Travel up to 30%. My partner's work is in San Diego, so this one is fixed.

## How I work

- I log every deal I lose and send product the technical reasons, with data, within a week.
