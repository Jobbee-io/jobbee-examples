**The 94% year, in full.** In March 2017 Bramblewood restructured its comp plan mid-year (new accelerator thresholds, grandfathered nobody) and, separately, discontinued the self-serve tier that produced 31% of my book's inbound. Two of my three biggest open deals died in legal within the same quarter — one to a budget freeze, one because the customer's own CISO killed the category. I finished at 94%, the only sub-100 year of my career, and I stayed through the rebuild instead of jumping mid-slump. What it changed permanently: I now read a comp plan's *change history* before I read its headline, I keep 2.5× pipeline coverage instead of 2.0×, and I never let more than 30% of my book depend on one tier or one buying motion. The two things that made me better as a seller — the plan-reading habit and the coverage discipline — both came from that year, which is the honest argument for keeping it in the record.
