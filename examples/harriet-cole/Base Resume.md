## How I count outcomes

Consulting resumes blur the number a deck promised and the number a business got. I keep 4 stages apart, and every outcome below says which one it is.

- Identified: in our business case or recommendation.
- Approved: signed off by the client's executive team or board.
- Implemented: live in the business (rotas changed, contracts ended, roles filled).
- Realised: visible in the client's numbers against a baseline agreed in advance, and signed off by client finance.

Run-rate means annualised at the end of the stated period. "I" means work I did or decided myself. "We" means the engagement team. Client staff did the implementing, and each bullet says where.
