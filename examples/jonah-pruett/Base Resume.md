## How I define the numbers in this file

- Activation at Slatebird (since January 2025): a new workspace where people create at least 5 assignments on the schedule, not counting ones created by templates or imports, and at least 2 people are active, within 7 days of signup. Before January 2025 it was "at least 5 assignments of any origin". That gap is the story in `Projects/Template Onboarding False Win.md`.
- Activation at Dispatchwell: a trial account that schedules at least 3 jobs and dispatches at least 1 of them to a technician's phone (which can be the owner's own) within 14 days of signup.
- Trial-to-paid: the share of trial signups that become a paying account within 60 days of signup (45 days at Dispatchwell).
- Paid CAC: paid media spend plus paid-channel tools and contractors in a period, divided by new paying accounts whose signup came from a paid click (last paid click, 30-day window) in the same period. Salaries are not in it, and I say so whenever I quote it.
- Payback, in months: paid CAC divided by (starting MRR of a new paid account × 82% gross margin). It's simple payback, with no churn in it. When a decision is close I also show the cohort version with churn.

- Paid acquisition restructure (2023): ran a geo holdout on brand search (about 82% of brand-paid signups came anyway), cut brand spend from $1.6M to $0.4M a year, moved bidding from signups to activated trials, and rebuilt 140 campaigns into 22.
