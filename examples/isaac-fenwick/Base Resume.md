## Experience

### Rosterwell — Senior Product Designer (Apr 2023 – present); Product Designer (Feb 2022 – Mar 2023)

Booking, scheduling and payments software for fitness and wellness studios: about 8,500 studios in the US and Canada, a studio web app, a member mobile app (iOS and Android), and an Enterprise tier for brands with many locations. 12 product designers when I joined, 14 now; one UX researcher until June 2025, two since. My teams: Studio Growth (Feb 2022 – Mar 2023), Member App (Apr – Dec 2023), Enterprise (Jan 2024 – Jun 2025), and design lead across Member App and Studio Settings since Jul 2025.

- **Studio onboarding redesign (Mar – Oct 2022).** Only designer on Studio Growth. Rebuilt trial setup around the schedule instead of payment verification. Activation went from 31.4% to 39.8% in a 6-week A/B test, +8.4 points (95% CI +4.2 to +12.6), and averaged about 39% over the next 6 months. Activation means that within 14 days of signup the studio published at least 7 days of classes and took a booking from someone not on its staff. This work got me promoted to Senior. Full notes: `Projects/Studio Onboarding Redesign.md`.
- **Class schedule redesign, rolled back (Jun – Aug 2023).** Led a card-style redesign of the member app's schedule screen. It lost: bookings per weekly active member −3.8% (95% CI −4.6% to −3.0%) in a 3-week A/B test, and −5.9% among regular members. Rolled back on Aug 30, 2023. A denser follow-up shipped in Dec 2023 at +1.2% (95% CI +0.4% to +2.0%). Full notes: `Projects/Class Schedule Rollback.md`.
- **Enterprise admin console redesign (Feb 2024 – Jun 2025).** Design lead with one other designer, a PM and 7 engineers. Turned a single-location console into one built for brands with 15 to 400 locations: make a change once, choose which locations it applies to, and preview the difference. Median time on 5 benchmark tasks fell 69% to 87% (8 admins per round), task success went from 70% to 95%, and how-do-I tickets per 100 locations fell 61%. Full notes: `Projects/Enterprise Admin Console.md`.
- **Thursday usability sessions (Jan 2024 – present).** Set up a weekly testing slot with our researcher: three remote sessions every Thursday, open to any team. Features tested with users before build went from 22% in 2023 to 81% in 2025. Full notes: `Projects/Thursday Usability Sessions.md`.
- **Waitlists and late-cancel policies (Jul 2025 – present).** Design lead across the member app and studio settings teams. Automatic waitlist promotion with a 2-hour claim window shipped in Mar 2026. The share of waitlisted members who got a spot went from 22% to 37% (Apr – Sep 2026 against the same months of 2025; no A/B test, so anything else that changed that year is in the number too).
- **Hiring and mentoring.** On the design hiring panel since 2023: screened about 300 resumes and ran about 45 portfolio reviews. Changed the portfolio review from a showreel to one project walked in depth: decisions, numbers, and what failed. Mentored 3 designers; 2 were promoted (2024, 2026).
- Tools: Figma and FigJam, ProtoPie for mobile prototypes, Amplitude for funnels, basic SQL to check a query an analyst wrote.

## Failures, indexed honestly

- The class schedule redesign (2023). It looked better and booked worse. I tested it with new members while regular members make about 70% of bookings.
- The 84-location price change in the admin console beta (2024). The button said "Apply" when it should have said how many locations.
- The spreadsheet import that assumed one file format (2022). Support tickets per trial rose 9% until we added the second format.
- Corbel's capacity data (2020). I built slot picking on data I never checked; 6 shops were double-booked in the first two weeks.
- Pitching the Thursday sessions to the design team before I'd talked to our researcher (2023). It was her field, and she heard about it secondhand.
